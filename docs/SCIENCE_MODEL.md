# Scientific model

## The neutral spine

The base model is a causal sequence, not one universal list of “fitness qualities”:

1. Goal and session intent
2. Task and prescribed dose
3. Performed external work
4. Internal response and mechanical/tissue exposure
5. Acute response and recovery state
6. Repeated exposure and chronic adaptation
7. Task-specific capability
8. Test or sport performance

The system must say whether a statement is intended, prescribed, performed, measured, estimated,
inferred from evidence or actually observed over time.

## Eight developer-facing lenses

| Id | UI name | Question |
|---|---|---|
| `coaching` | Coaching | What are we trying to develop or protect today? |
| `sport_activity` | Sport & activity | What sport and exact task is this? |
| `method_format` | Method & format | How is the work performed and organized? |
| `performance_skill` | Performance & skill | What task-specific ability or skill might this support? |
| `mechanics` | Mechanics | What force, velocity, power or movement quantity is known? |
| `physiology` | Physiology | What energy, recruitment, fibre or adaptation statement is justified? |
| `body_tissue` | Body & tissue | Where is the exposure expressed? |
| `work_response_recovery` | Work, response & recovery | What was done, how did the athlete respond and what is their current state? |

These are stable navigation concepts. The larger vocabulary beneath them should remain
data-driven and versioned.

## Terms that must stay distinct

| Term | Scientific role |
|---|---|
| Fast/slow fibre phenotype | Muscle- and measurement-specific biological observation |
| Hypertrophy | Chronic structural adaptation |
| Strength | Task-specific performance capability |
| Overall strength | Transparent composite of selected tests |
| Power | Mechanical quantity: force × velocity |
| Rate of force development | Change in force over time |
| Impulse | Force integrated over time |
| Reactive strength | Rapid stretch-shortening-cycle capability |
| Explosiveness | Friendly composite label, not one indivisible trait |
| Plyometrics | Training method |
| Strength day | Coaching/session intent |
| Olympic weightlifting | Sport/discipline |
| EMOM | Workout format |

## Coaching frameworks

Named systems such as Garage Strength/Parabolic Periodization should be stored as versioned
frameworks. Their phases and day archetypes map to neutral concepts using qualified
relationships.

For example, “Impulse Day” is a framework label. It may relate to sprinting, acceleration or
rapid-force capabilities, but it does not mean biomechanical impulse was measured.

## Required scientific guardrails

- No universal cross-sport training-load total.
- No whole-person fibre-type percentage inferred from performance.
- No exclusive energy-system labels; contributions overlap and change over time.
- No treating strength, hypertrophy, power, rate of force development and impulse as synonyms.
- No treating a method such as plyometrics as an adaptation.
- No assuming one activity has only one purpose.
- No duplicating one performed dose across every supported goal.
- No equating intended stimulus with exposure, response or observed adaptation.
- No static “exercise causes outcome” claim without dose and context.
- No converting unknown into zero.
- No anatomy or injury certainty from an exercise name alone.
- No rewriting historical projections when a scientific model changes.

## Anatomy and tissue

Anatomy should support region, joint, structure, tissue, laterality, joint action, range of
motion and contraction type. Tissue exposure may consider force/strain magnitude, rate,
repetitions, contacts and impacts.

External load is an input, not automatically tissue load. A barbell load, jump count or ground
reaction force needs an explicit estimation model before it becomes a tissue-exposure assertion.

## Evidence-backed relationships

Prefer contextual claims:

> Under this dose, execution style, population and timeframe, repeated exposure may contribute
> to this capability, with this evidence and uncertainty.

Avoid unconditional claims:

> This exercise develops this outcome.

Projection history must retain its formula/model version and input lineage.
